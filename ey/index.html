How EY builds coherent applications faster, for itself and for its clients

EY Caribbean's Technology Consulting practice uses Think4Ever to build its own internal applications and to deliver client software projects, with business intent, policies and acceptance criteria carried from the first conversation to released code.

The challenge

Consulting firms are being asked to deliver software faster than ever, and AI coding tools have made writing code dramatically quicker. But speed at the keyboard has not solved the harder problem: making sure what gets built still matches what the business actually needs.

EY faced this on two fronts. Internally, teams needed applications to support internal need, e.g. engagement management, knowledge workflows, reporting. On client engagements, EY delivery teams regularly inherit existing systems whose architecture, dependencies and business rules are only partly documented, and where critical knowledge sits with a handful of people.

  • Context lost in handoffs. Business objectives, policies and acceptance criteria were captured in slides and workshops, then drifted as work moved from advisory to delivery teams.
  • Slow discovery. Understanding an existing system before changing it took X weeks per engagement and had to be repeated whenever teams changed.
  • Rework and misalignment. Faster AI-assisted coding increased the risk of shipping changes that were technically correct but misaligned with approved intent.
  • Limited capability transfer. Clients increasingly want to keep the capability after the engagement ends, not just receive a delivered system.

Why EY chose Think4Ever

EY selected Think4Ever because it treats governed business context, not just code, as the foundation of delivery. The platform gives teams three things that matter in consulting work.

Clarity

Existing code, workflows, objectives, policies and constraints are visible in one reviewable Living Blueprint that clients and EY teams review together.

Coherence

Applications and changes are built from approved intent, so implementation stays aligned as teams and AI tools move faster.

Control

People stay in charge of decisions, approvals and governance, and choose which tools or agents execute the work, inside Think4Ever or in the coding agents teams already use.

The approach: one governed loop

EY teams apply the same four-step method to internal builds and to client projects.

StepStageWhat EY teams do
1. UnderstandCode → DesignMake the existing architecture, workflows, dependencies and known rules reviewable, working with the people who know them.
2. EncodeBusiness contextCapture objectives, policies, guardrails, decisions and acceptance criteria as approved intent.
3. BuildDesign → CodeCreate applications and changes in Think4Ever or supported coding agents, using the same reviewed context.
4. GovernRelease and learnTest, approve and release; review coherence against intent and keep the Living Blueprint current.

The speed does not come from skipping understanding. It comes from encoding the important business and system context once, reviewing it, and reusing it across every application and change.
